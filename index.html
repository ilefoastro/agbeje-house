About Agbeje House

Agbeje House is a family business in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. We bake and fry fresh snacks, cook for events, and decorate the venue, so that one team can look after your food and your setting.

Our recipes come from a family that has worked with flour for generations: our grandmother, our mother, and now us.

We make puff puff, buns, small chops, chin chin, fish pie and more, and we serve drinks such as sobo (zobo) and juice. For funerals, birthdays, anniversaries, naming ceremonies, festivals and meetings, we bring the food and, if you want, the decoration too.

How we work: you tell us the event, the date and the number of guests. We suggest a menu and a decoration plan, confirm the price with you, and agree everything before you pay. We would rather answer your questions first than surprise you on the day.
